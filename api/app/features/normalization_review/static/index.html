            Normalization decisions

            How a vehicle receives its status

            The percentage is a routing confidence level, not the mathematical probability that every field is correct. Status is determined by the strongest unresolved condition found in the record.

            95%

            Resolved

            No review reason, candidate value, or provisional model-family signal remains. Accepted rules produced the result.

            80%

            Provisional

            No blocking conflict exists, but at least one field is still a candidate or the model family needs corroboration.

            55%

            Needs review

            At least one explicit review reason exists—for example missing manufacturer evidence, conflicting manufacturer evidence, or an unresolved fuel/bodywork interpretation.

            0%

            Failed

            The source record is structurally invalid and could not be processed safely.

            Routing order

            The first applicable safety condition wins

            1. 1
              Can the record be read?No → Failed · 0%
            2. 2
              Does any rule produce a review reason?Yes → Needs review · 55%
            3. 3
              Are candidate values or a provisional model family present?Yes → Provisional · 80%
            4. 4
              Only accepted normalized values remainResolved · 95%
            What sends a car to review

            Review reasons are explicit

            • Manufacturer is missing and Brand cannot safely confirm it.
            • Manufacturer, Brand, base manufacturer, or entity evidence conflicts.
            • A bodywork code is unresolved for the vehicle category.
            • Fuel fields describe an inconsistent carrier combination.
            • A marketing term lacks the required manufacturer or category scope.
            How review is completed

            Add evidence, then reprocess

            • Approve a manufacturer entity or translation rule.
            • Confirm the vehicle through VIN evidence or TecDoc KType.
            • Correct an incorrect rule or source classification.
            • Re-import into a new immutable batch and compare the status totals.
